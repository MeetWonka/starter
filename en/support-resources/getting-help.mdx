There are three ways to get help with WonkaChat: search this help center, use the in-app Help & FAQ, or contact support directly.

## In-app help

Every WonkaChat workspace has a **Help & FAQ** entry in the left sidebar. It links you straight to this help center without leaving the app.

## Before you contact support

A good support ticket gets a faster, more accurate answer. Include:

<Steps>
<Step title="What you were doing">
The task or workflow you were trying to complete, step by step if possible.
</Step>

<Step title="The error message or a screenshot">
Copy the exact error text, or attach a screenshot. Avoid paraphrasing error messages — small details matter for diagnosis.
</Step>

<Step title="Your browser">
Which browser and version you're using (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
</Step>

<Step title="Approximate time it happened">
The date and time (with time zone) helps us find the relevant logs.
</Step>

<Step title="Your organization name">
So we can locate your workspace and check organization-specific settings.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
If the issue involves a specific connector (email, CRM, storage, etc.), mention which one — most connection issues have a quick fix. See [Troubleshooting Connections](/en/tools-connection/troubleshooting-connections).
</Tip>
