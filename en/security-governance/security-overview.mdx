This page explains the protections built into WonkaChat. For certifications, hosting and model regions, see [Compliance & governance](/en/security-governance/compliance-and-governance).

## Sign-in and sessions

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Sign-in options" icon="right-to-bracket">
- **Email and password.** Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text.
- **Single sign-on (SSO).** Administrators can connect your identity provider with OpenID Connect (Microsoft, Google or a custom provider) or SAML.
- **Require SSO for non-admin users.** When SSO is on, administrators can force everyone except organization admins to sign in through SSO.

Setup steps: [SSO and 2FA](/en/administration/sso-and-2fa).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Two-factor authentication (2FA)" icon="mobile-screen">
Each user can turn on two-factor authentication in **Settings → Account → Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**. After that, sign-in asks for a code from your one-time password app.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Session tokens" icon="key">
WonkaChat uses short-lived access tokens (15 minutes) and refresh tokens (7 days). After 7 days without a refresh, you sign in again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Rate limiting" icon="gauge">
Login, registration, password reset and API calls are rate-limited to slow down brute-force and abuse.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Your data

<CardGroup cols={2}>
<Card title="Isolated per organization" icon="building-lock">
Conversations, agents, files, connectors and settings belong to your organization. Other organizations cannot see them.
</Card>

<Card title="Encrypted" icon="lock">
Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256).
</Card>

<Card title="Not used for training" icon="ban">
Your data is not used to train public AI models.
</Card>

<Card title="Hosted in the EU" icon="server">
WonkaChat runs on Microsoft Azure West Europe by default.
</Card>
</CardGroup>

## How AI models are reached

When you send a message, WonkaChat forwards it to the model you selected through an AI gateway. The gateway connects to model providers such as Microsoft Azure, Google Vertex AI, Mistral and others.

Each model runs in a specific region: the EU, the US, or a global endpoint. **Data you send to a model is processed in that model's region.** The model selector shows a region badge next to each model. See [Model regions](/en/security-governance/compliance-and-governance#model-regions).

## Connectors

Connectors (MCP) let agents read from and act in your tools, such as email, files or CRM.

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Your permissions, never more" icon="user-lock">
A connector acts with **your** account in the connected tool. If you cannot see or do something in that tool yourself, the agent cannot either.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Personal credentials" icon="id-card">
When you connect a tool, the connection is stored for your account only. Other users in your organization cannot use your credentials.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Custom connectors" icon="plug">
If your organization allows it, users can add custom connectors in **Customize → Connectors**. WonkaChat labels them: *Custom connectors are not verified by Wonka AI.* Add only servers you trust.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Administrator control" icon="sliders">
Administrators decide which users and groups can use each connector in **Settings → Organization → Connector access**. See [Groups and tool policies](/en/administration/groups-and-tool-policies).
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
A connector can do whatever its actions allow with your account, including sending emails or deleting files. In the agent builder, turn off the actions an agent does not need, or switch the connector to **Read-only**. See [Access control](/en/security-governance/access-control#tool-restriction).
</Warning>
