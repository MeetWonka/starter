---
title: "Administration"
description: "Manage your WonkaChat organization: users, security, groups, connector access and usage."
---

Organization admins and owners manage everything organization-wide from **Settings → Organization**. This section covers each area in detail.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
<Card title="Organization settings" icon="building" href="/en/administration/organization-settings">
User management and invites, the organization system prompt, workspace tags, subscription, and per-feature permissions.
</Card>

<Card title="SSO and 2FA" icon="key" href="/en/administration/sso-and-2fa">
Set up single sign-on (OIDC or SAML), require SSO for non-admin users, and enable two-factor authentication.
</Card>

<Card title="Groups and tool policies" icon="people-group" href="/en/administration/groups-and-tool-policies">
Create groups for easier sharing, and control which users or groups can use each connector.
</Card>

<Card title="Audit log" icon="file-lines" href="/en/administration/audit-log">
Review every access change in your organization, and audit what your agents do if enabled.
</Card>

<Card title="Usage and stats" icon="chart-line" href="/en/administration/usage-and-stats">
Track your own usage and, as an admin, your organization's and agents' usage.
</Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
Some settings are visible only to the organization **owner** (User Management), only to admins with the right permission (Security, Tool policies, Groups), or only when Wonka AI has enabled a feature for your organization. Each page below says which.
</Note>
