## My Stats

Every user can review their own usage from **Settings → My Stats**: a daily usage chart, a summary of tokens and cost, a ranked list of most-used agents and models, and a transaction log. Filter by date range (**Today**, current month, previous month, or a custom range), by agent, or by model.

## Organization Stats

<Note>
Settings → Organization Stats. Visible to admins and owners.
</Note>

Organization Stats shows the same charts and tables for your whole organization: total usage, cost, and a breakdown by user, agent and model. If you own several organizations, switch between them from the organization selector at the top.

<Info>
Clickable, interactive filters (drilling into a specific user or agent from the chart) are available when your organization has the **interactive usage-stats filters** feature enabled. Without it, use the date range and dropdown filters instead.
</Info>

## Agent Stats

<Note>
Settings → Agent Stats. Visible to agent editors and owners.
</Note>

Agent Stats focuses on a single agent: its own usage chart, cost summary, model breakdown and transaction log, filterable by date range and model. Pick the agent from the selector at the top of the page.

## Balance and credits

If your organization has a usage budget, your remaining balance or credits are shown in the relevant stats pages and in warnings when you're close to or past a limit. Admins set organization- and per-user usage limits from **Settings → Organization → User Management** (see [Organization settings](/en/administration/organization-settings)).

<Tip>
If you see a message that your organization's free trial has ended or its balance is exhausted, an admin needs to subscribe or top up from **Settings → Organization → Subscription**.
</Tip>
