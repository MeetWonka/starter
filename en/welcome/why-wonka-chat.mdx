<Tabs>
<Tab title="Standard and advanced models">

- Strategic recommendations
- Multi-step workflow planning
- Long document analysis

- "Analyze our Q3 sales data and recommend territory adjustments"
- "Create a comprehensive onboarding plan for new employees"
- "Review this contract and identify potential risks"
</Tab>

<Tab title="Fast models">
**Best for:** Quick answers, simple tasks, fast responses, and high-volume operations

**Use when you need:**
- Rapid information retrieval
- Simple formatting or editing
- Straightforward questions
- Quick summaries
- High-volume operations

**Example tasks:**
- "What's on my calendar today?"
- "Send a meeting confirmation to the team"
- "Summarize this email thread"

<Warning>
Fast models are excellent for quick, straightforward tasks, but they don't have reasoning capabilities and aren't a good fit for complex system usage (ERP, CRM, and similar tools). Consider the task requirements before choosing this mode.
</Warning>
</Tab>

## External Tool and Data Connections via MCP

### Model Context Protocol (MCP)

WonkaChat uses **MCP (Model Context Protocol)** to connect AI models to your business tools and software systems.

<Info>
**What is MCP?** MCP is an open protocol that enables secure, standardized connections between AI models and external tools. It lets WonkaChat interact with your emails, calendars, databases, CRMs, and other business applications while maintaining security and permission boundaries.
</Info>

**Key MCP capabilities:**
- Direct integration with business systems (Outlook, Gmail, Odoo, Notion, and more)
- Secure credential management and authentication
- Real-time data access and write operations
- Permission inheritance from your existing user access
- Custom connectors your organization can add, subject to admin-controlled tool policies

<Tip>
For comprehensive information about MCP architecture, supported integrations, connection setup, and advanced configuration options, visit the [Tools & Connection](/en/tools-connection/introduction) section.
</Tip>

## Model Flexibility, Explained

### Sizes, Not Names

Instead of asking you to track dozens of model names, WonkaChat groups models by what they're good at:

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Model sizes">
- **Fast models** — lightweight and quick, best for simple, high-volume requests without deep reasoning.
- **Standard models** — balanced speed and reasoning for everyday professional work.
- **Advanced reasoning models** — the deepest analysis for complex, multi-step, or high-stakes tasks.
</Tab>

<Tab title="Selection criteria">
**Factors to consider when choosing a model size:**

1. **Task complexity** — advanced models for reasoning and tool-heavy work, fast models for quick, simple tasks.
2. **Region** — each model in the selector shows a region badge; see [Security & Governance](/en/security-governance/security-overview) for details.
3. **Organization policy** — administrators can restrict which models or sizes are available to your team.
</Tab>

<Tab title="Switching strategy">
**Benefits of switching between sizes:**

- **Match cost to task** — save advanced reasoning for the work that needs it.
- **Speed when it matters** — use fast models for high-volume, low-complexity requests.
- **Change anytime** — switch models mid-conversation as your task evolves.
</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
Model access can be configured per organization, per team, or per individual user based on your governance policies. Full details are in [Choosing a Model](/en/improved-usage/choosing-a-model).
</Tip>
