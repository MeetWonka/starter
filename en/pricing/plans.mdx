## AI Workspace seat prices

Seats are priced in bands: the first 50 seats are billed at the first rate, the next 200 at the second rate, and so on. Annual billing saves 20%.

| Seats | With AI models included, monthly | With AI models included, annual | Own API key, monthly | Own API key, annual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First 50 | €27.00 | €21.60 | €20.00 | €16.00 |
| Next 200 (51–250) | €23.00 | €18.40 | €16.00 | €12.80 |
| Next 750 (251–1,000) | €19.00 | €15.20 | €12.00 | €9.60 |
| 1,000+ | Enterprise | Enterprise | Enterprise | Enterprise |

<Tip>
**Example:** 100 seats with AI models included, billed monthly = 50 × €27 + 50 × €23 = **€2,500 per month**. Billed annually, the same team pays **€2,000 per month**.
</Tip>

## AI models: included or your own key

The AI Workspace seat price depends on how AI models are provided. Pick the option that fits your organization.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
<Card title="AI models included" icon="sparkles">
EU-hosted AI models with no usage bills. Each seat includes €7 in AI credits. This is the simplest option: one predictable price.
</Card>

<Card title="Your own API key" icon="key">
Connect your own model provider API key. Your AI usage is billed directly by your provider, and the seat price is €7 lower.

## Frequently asked questions

Yes. The free trial lasts 7 days, includes full AI Workspace access and €5 of AI usage, and requires no credit card. [Start here](https://wonka.chat/register).

<Accordion title="How does the seat discount work?" icon="layer-group">
The price per seat drops as your team grows, band by band. For example, with 60 seats, the first 50 are billed at the first-band rate and the 10 others at the second-band rate.
