Every time you send a message to WonkaChat or an agent, an AI model reads your request and writes an answer. That work is measured in **tokens**. How tokens are billed depends on your plan.

## What are tokens?

Tokens are the units that measure AI model usage. Both your question and the AI's answer use tokens.

- 1 token ≈ 3/4 of a word in English
- "Hello, how are you?" ≈ 6 tokens
- A typical email (200 words) ≈ 265 tokens
- A full page of text (500 words) ≈ 665 tokens

Files, long conversations, and tool results (for example a web search or a connected app) also add tokens, because the model has to read them.

## Why some models use more credits than others

AI models come in different sizes. Larger models are better at complex reasoning, but each token costs more.

| Model size | Good for | Relative cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fast / small | Simple questions, classification, short summaries, high-volume tasks | Low |
| Standard | Emails, reports, research, most day-to-day work | Medium |
| Advanced / reasoning | Complex analysis, strategy, coding, critical decisions | High |

The models available to you are listed in the model selector in WonkaChat. Your administrator decides which models your organization can use.

## Tips to use credits well

- **Start with a standard model.** Move up to an advanced model only when the answer quality is not good enough.
- **Start a new conversation for a new topic.** Long conversations make every new message more expensive, because the model re-reads the history.
- **Attach only the files you need.** Every attached document is read by the model.
- **Give each agent the right model.** A simple FAQ agent does not need the most advanced model.
